In this world you build the Kit Mod, a Factorio mod that adds a new alloy, a chest made from it and the research that unlocks both. You do not need to have modded before. First, the five pieces the game is made of, because a mod adds more of exactly these.

**Items.** An **item** is a thing you can carry: an iron plate, a gear, a chest folded into your pocket. Items sit in inventories and ride on belts, and many of the same item share one inventory slot, called a **stack**.

**Entities.** An **entity** is a thing that stands on the map: a placed chest, an assembling machine, a tree, a biter. Placing a chest turns the chest item in your hand into a chest entity on the ground, and mining it turns it back. Same name, two different things.

**Recipes.** A **recipe** turns items into other items over a few seconds: 2 iron plates become 1 gear in half a second. The recipe calculator from the last world was a pile of these.

**Technologies.** Most recipes start locked. A **technology** is a research project, and finishing it unlocks recipes. Many technologies need others finished first, which is what makes the tech tree.

**Science packs and labs.** Research is paid in **science packs**: items you craft and feed into a **lab**, an entity that uses them up while it researches. The red automation science pack is the first one every player makes.

**Why this matters.** The game reads a description of every item, entity, recipe and technology when it starts. A mod adds new descriptions or changes old ones, and that is how the Kit Mod will put its alloy, chest and research into the game.
