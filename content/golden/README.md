# Golden quests

Hand-written samples of the lesson shape every quest should have. Not loaded by the app
(only `content/tracks` is); they exist to be read and copied.

The shape:

- **One idea per quest.** `let` is a quest. `mut` is the next one. A term the exercise does not
  need is not in the lesson.
- **Situation, then it happens, then the name.** The learner sees the thing (12 logs, a red
  error) before they get the word for it (variable, immutable). Each word is introduced once,
  in bold, with a plain-English gloss in the same sentence.
- **Every paragraph is one card.** One thought, two or three sentences, at most one line of code
  inside it. The single code block is the thing the learner will type, nothing extra.
- **The compiler is a character, not an obstacle.** Where a mistake is the natural first move,
  the lesson has the learner make it and read the message.
- **Helpers the exercise needs but the lesson is not about** are already written in the
  starter, or named in the `// TODO:` comment. They do not become lesson bullets.
- **Your turn** is 2-4 steps a beginner can follow literally, naming the function and the line.
- **Hints** do not repeat the lesson. Hint 1 says where; the last hint says what to type.
- **Points** ask for the one thing the card above just showed, in its own words.
