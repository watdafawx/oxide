# Content review, 2026-10-19

Read-only review of every quest in `content/worlds/**` (Rust, 123 quests) and `content/tracks/lua/**` (12 quests) against `docs/CONTENT.md`. The 67 `flavours/games` overlays were read next to their plain quest. Solutions were skimmed only to judge hints. Nothing in `content/` was edited.

Scope note: the brief said Lua had 3 quests. Another session committed Lua 04-12 (`15b0f2c`) while this review ran, so all 12 are covered. `content/tracks/java/` (one untracked setup quest) was not reviewed.

Method: every `quest.toml`, `lesson.md` and starter was read. A script checked ranges, lesson length, `Your turn` bullets, TODO coverage, Cargo name uniqueness, and whether identifiers in hints, labels and `Your turn` exist in the starter. It found no mismatches, so **hints and labels match each flavour's names**. Nothing was compiled or run. The review did not run `content-check`.

Severity: **[H]** hurts a learner in the first hour or is plainly wrong. **[M]** real friction or spec miss. **[L]** polish.

---

## 1. Summary

1. 135 quests reviewed (123 Rust, 12 Lua) plus 67 games flavours; 196 tagged findings in section 4 (11 [H], 80 [M], 105 [L]); many [L] are test-name leaks counted per quest.
2. The games flavours are strong where they exist: all 67 open with a bold **Why now**, every `Your turn` names the right file and function, and hints and labels use the flavour's names.
3. The biggest systemic problem: **60 of 60 games exercise and boss quests keep the plain test names** (`circle_area_uses_pi` for `ItemKind::Tool`, `dog_describes_itself` for `Zombie`). They show in the run console and failure text.
4. Worlds 07-11 (55 quests) and Lua have no games flavour and no Why-now opener. Their lessons are textbook-named. With games as the default, the app switches voice at world 07.
5. Tutorial quests 02 and 03 have no games flavour either, so a learner on the default flavour hits plain text in minutes 4-10, and the tutorial boss (100 xp, 5 min, 2 objectives) is outside every boss range.
6. Sizing: 6 of 12 Rust worlds open with a first quest of 5-6 minutes (spec: under 5). Five exercises ask for 5+ functions or edits (03/08, 05/07, Lua 03, 04 and 09); the hardest early spike is 01/04 (4 functions, 12 min, 65 xp).
7. Rhythm: worlds 03, 05, 08, 10 and 11 stack 4-5 quests of 10-15 minutes and 55-80 xp with no quick win between them. Worlds 00, 01, 02, 06, 07 and Lua are fine.
8. Lesson length is healthy: 128 of 135 plain lessons and 64 of 67 games lessons are 35 lines or fewer (blank lines included). Over: `09-systems/10` (55), `10-apps/11` (39), `06-smart-pointers/02` (38), plus four at 37 and two at 36.
9. Teaching-order bugs: the 03 boss needs `?` before 04/01 teaches it; `06/09` needs threads before world 07; `02/08` writes lifetimes a quest before the lifetimes quest; `09/09` prints the full solution in the lesson.
10. Mechanically clean: Cargo names unique across the catalog, `edition = "2024"` and `[workspace]` everywhere, no external crate outside its topic, every starter checked has `TODO` comments (nested proc-macro crates included). Hints escalate except where listed. Bosses in worlds 04, 06, 08, 09, 10 and 11 have only 3 hints for 7-8 objectives.

## 4. Every flagged item, by quest

Ids are shortened: `01/04` means `worlds/01-foundations/04-if-and-loops`. "G" means the games flavour, "P" the plain one. Where a finding applies to every games quest it is listed once under **Systemic** and only named quests with an extra problem appear again.

### Systemic

- [H] **Plain test names in games flavours** (60 quests). Spec says objective test names are identical across flavours, so this is a spec/engine consequence, but learners see `tests::circle_area_uses_pi ... FAILED` in a game about tools. Worst: 01/08, 01/12, 05/01, 05/10, 03/09, 02/10, 06/08, 04/08, 04/11. See the fixes in section 2.
- [M] **No games flavour for worlds 07-11, Lua, and tutorial 02-03.** `CONTENT.md` calls the games flavour optional, but "games by default" means these show plain text with no Why-now.
- [M] **Plain lessons have no labelled "why this matters" opener** (spec: a short real-world one). About half open with a pure definition (e.g. 01/09, 01/10, 02/04, 03/07, 03/10, 04/08, 05/03, 05/07, 05/09, 06/02). Only Lua 04-12 use a labelled Why now.
- [M] **Boss hints**: worlds 04, 06, 08, 09, 10 and 11 bosses have 3 hints for 7-8 objectives, and the third hint packs several functions into one paragraph. 01, 02, 03, 05, 07 and Lua bosses have 4-5.
- [M] **Folder slugs and Cargo names keep plain words** in games worlds: `04/11-boss-todo-cli` (`err_boss_todo`), `05/12-boss-shape-renderer` (`trait_boss_shape_renderer`), `06/10-boss-tree-editor`, `03/11-boss-csv-stats`. Invisible to learners if titles are used; visible in ids and workspace folders.
- [L] **First-build cost is not in the minutes** for tokio, rayon, rusqlite (bundled C), egui, tauri; only 07/09 warns.
- [L] **Several tests are timing-based** (08/02 about 300 ms vs 600 ms, 08/06, 08/09 "within milliseconds", 07/11 10 s guard) and can flake on a loaded machine.
- [L] **Hints give the whole answer too early** in many quests (01/10 hint 1, 01/12 hints, 02/01 hint 3, 03/09 hint 2). Acceptable as a *last* hint, but several sit in the middle.

## 5. What is working (keep doing it)

- Every games lesson starts with a concrete **Why now**, and the best ones tie to a feature (`02/05` slices, `05/05` dispatch, `05/12` hitbox, `06/05` refcell).
- Hints in games flavours use the flavour's own names; none refers to a plain-named function.
- Lua 04-12 and the Rust bosses of worlds 01, 02, 03 and 05 are well staged: the first objective is a one-liner, order is stated, and `Press Run` bosses ship a `main`.
- Reads (quizzes) are short and use project vocabulary in games flavours.
- Tests use `assert_eq!` with messages, temp dirs are pid-unique, loopback sockets use port 0, and channel tests use timeouts so mistakes fail instead of freezing.
